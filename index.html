    Transport matters

    Go Electric, Go Green 🚗⚡

    Transport is one of the biggest sources of personal emissions. Electric vehicles have no tailpipe emissions and are cheaper to run. Even switching a few trips a week to public transport, cycling or carpooling makes a visible difference.

    • No tailpipe smoke in your city
    • Lower running cost per km
    • Even better when charged with solar ☀️
    Small changes

    Quick Eco Tips

    Small changes that make a real difference.

    💧 Save water

    Fix leaking taps and switch off the tap while brushing.

    ☀️ Go solar

    A rooftop solar panel can cut your electricity bill and your CO₂.

    🌬️ Choose renewables

    Pick green energy plans and efficient (5-star) appliances.

    🌱 Plant a tree

    One tree absorbs roughly 20 kg of CO₂ every year.

    ♻️ Segregate waste

    Separate wet and dry waste so more of it can be recycled.

    🛡️ Protect nature

    Avoid single-use plastic and keep parks and rivers clean.

    Did you know? 💡

    LED bulbs use up to 80% less energy than old bulbs, and unplugging idle chargers stops "phantom" power use. Small things add up!

    Doubts?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a carbon footprint?

    It is the total amount of greenhouse gases, mainly CO₂, produced by your daily activities like travel, electricity use, cooking and food.

    Are the results 100% accurate?

    No. EcoPulse gives approximate estimates (based on typical India emission factors) to create awareness, not exact scientific measurements.

    Is my data saved anywhere?

    Your habits, challenge and results are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. There is no login, no sign-up and no cost. Just open the page and start.

    How can I reduce my footprint?

    Use public transport, save electricity, reduce waste and eat more plant-based meals. Check the Tips and What-if sections above.
