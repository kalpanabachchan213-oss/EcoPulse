Go Electric, Go Green 🚗⚡

Transport is one of the biggest sources of personal emissions.

Why electric vehicles?

Electric vehicles produce no tailpipe emissions and are cheaper to run. Even switching a few trips a week to public transport, cycling or carpooling makes a visible difference to your monthly footprint.

Quick Eco Tips

Small changes that make a real difference.

💡

Switch to LED

LED bulbs use up to 80% less energy than old incandescent bulbs.

🚌

Use public transport

Bus, metro or carpooling cuts per-person emissions a lot.

🔌

Unplug idle devices

Chargers and TVs on standby keep drawing power.

♻️

Segregate waste

Separate wet and dry waste so more of it can be recycled.

💧

Save water

Fix leaking taps and turn off the tap while brushing.

🌱

Plant a tree

One tree absorbs roughly 20 kg of CO₂ every year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common doubts.

What is a carbon footprint?

It is the total amount of greenhouse gases, mainly CO₂, produced by your daily activities like travel, electricity use and food.

Are the results 100% accurate?

No. EcoPulse gives approximate estimates to create awareness, not exact scientific measurements.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Habit progress is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server.

How can I reduce my footprint?

Use public transport, save electricity, reduce waste and eat more plant-based meals. Check the Tips section above.
